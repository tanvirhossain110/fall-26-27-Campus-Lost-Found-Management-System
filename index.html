Who is Campus Lost & Found for?

Choose your type when you register. Everyone logs in from the same page.

Student

Students

  • Report items you have lost
  • Post items you have found
  • Send a claim with proof of ownership
  • Track the status of every claim
Register as Student
Faculty

Faculty

  • Report lost equipment or documents
  • Post items found in classrooms
  • Claim items belonging to your department
  • See the status of your claims
Register as Faculty
Officer

Lost & Found Officer

  • Keep the physical item inventory
  • Schedule collection appointments
  • Record every handover
  • Match lost reports with found items
Apply as Officer
Admin

Administrators

  • Manage users and roles

How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Register

Open Register, choose Student, Faculty or Officer, and fill in the details that type needs.

2

Log in

Use the single login page. Campus Lost & Found opens your own dashboard automatically. Officers log in after an admin approves them.

3

Use the service

Report, post, search and claim items, or manage the inventory, depending on your account.
